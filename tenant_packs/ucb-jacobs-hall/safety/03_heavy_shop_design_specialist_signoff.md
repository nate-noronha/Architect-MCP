# Mandatory Design Specialist / Shop Steward Check-Off (Room 122B)

Before powering on the **SawStop Table Saw**, **ShopBot PRSalpha CNC**, **OMAX 2626 Waterjet**, or **FabLight FL3000 Fiber Laser**, a certified Design Specialist or Shop Steward **MUST** sign off on:

1. **Workholding & Material Verification**: Sheet metal, plywood, or stock is clamped securely and verified against allowed materials (no tempered glass on the OMAX; no galvanized steel on the FabLight).
2. **CAM Toolpath & Dry Run**: CNC/waterjet/fiber-laser toolpaths, kerf offsets, and zero-points are verified prior to starting the cut.
3. **Consumables & Assist Gas**: OMAX garnet hopper level and FabLight assist gas pressure are confirmed by staff.

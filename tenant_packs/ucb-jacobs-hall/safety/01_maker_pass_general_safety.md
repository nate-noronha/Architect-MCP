# Jacobs Hall General Safety & Maker Pass Policy

1. **Active Maker Pass Required**: Every maker must hold an active semesterly UC Berkeley Maker Pass and have completed the online safety orientation before using tools or picking up Material Store orders.
2. **Mandatory Dress Code**: Closed-toe, closed-heel shoes and ANSI Z87.1 eye protection are mandatory in all fabrication zones. Long pants are required inside Basement Room 122B.
3. **Never Work Alone on Power Equipment**: Never operate table saws, CNC routers, waterjets, or metal lasers when staff or Design Specialists are not on duty.

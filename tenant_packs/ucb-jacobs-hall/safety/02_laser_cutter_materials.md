# Jacobs Hall Laser Cutter Safety & Approved Material Protocol

1. **Strictly Forbidden Materials**: Never cut **PVC, vinyl, polycarbonate, fiberglass, ABS, or carbon fiber** on the Universal Laser Systems cutters — they release corrosive hydrochloric acid or cyanide fumes and create severe fire hazards.
2. **Never Leave Unattended**: You must remain within arm's reach of the laser cutter for the entire duration of the cut with the exhaust blower running.
3. **Use Verified Stock**: Purchase verified laser-safe plywood and acrylic from the Jacobs Hall Material Store (`store.jacobshall.org`) or have a Design Specialist inspect outside stock.

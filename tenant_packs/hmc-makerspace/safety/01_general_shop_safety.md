# HMC MAKE & Shop General Safety Rules

1. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: Closed-toe shoes and ANSI Z87.1 rated safety glasses are mandatory across all fabrication zones.
2. **Buddy Rule**: Hazardous powered equipment in the Machine Shop and Wood Shop may NEVER be operated alone in the room. A certified Shop Steward or certified buddy must maintain line-of-sight.
3. **Zero Unapproved Modifications**: Never disable machine interlocks, blade guards, or laser lid switches.
4. **Incident Reporting**: Report any injury, smoke, blade contact, or broken tooling to a Shop Steward immediately via the MAKE Injury Report Form.

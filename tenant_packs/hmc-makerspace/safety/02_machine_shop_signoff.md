# Mandatory Steward Pre-Machining Check-Off

Before turning a spindle on the **Clausing Engine Lathe**, **Bridgeport Vertical Mill**, or **SawStop Table Saw**, the student **MUST** obtain an in-person Shop Steward sign-off verifying:

1. **Workholding & Fixturing**: Part is clamped solidly in vise/parallels or 3-jaw chuck with chuck key removed before touching the switch.
2. **Speeds & Feeds**: Spindle RPM is verified for material diameter and cutter type (e.g., 6061-T6 Aluminum vs. 304 Stainless Steel).
3. **Zero Gloves Hazard**: Hands and gloves are strictly banned near rotating chucks, arbors, and end mills to prevent entanglement.
4. **Clear Travel**: Carriage, quill, and table stops clear all clamps and DRO brackets.

# Laser Cutter Material & Attendance Policy

1. **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN MATERIALS**: Never cut **PVC, vinyl, polycarbonate, ABS, or unknown plastics**. PVC releases corrosive hydrochloric acid gas that destroys the optics and endangers lung health.
2. **100% Continuous Attendance**: Do not walk away from an active laser job for any reason. Keep your hand near the pause button to stop flare-ups.
3. **Approved Materials**: Cast acrylic (PMMA), Baltic birch plywood, Delrin (acetal — extra fume purge), anodized aluminum (marking only), and untreated leather.
4. **Post-Job Cooldown**: Wait 30 seconds before opening the lid so the exhaust fan clears residual fumes.

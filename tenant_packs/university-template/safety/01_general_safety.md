# General Campus Makerspace Safety Rules

1. **Mandatory PPE**: Safety glasses and closed-toe shoes are required at all times inside active fabrication zones.
2. **Buddy Rule**: Never operate power tools or rotating machinery alone in the lab.
3. **Steward Sign-Off**: Any operation on a mill, lathe, CNC router, or >50V electrical system requires an in-person Shop Steward check-off before powering on.

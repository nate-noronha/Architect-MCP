# Mandatory Mentor G-Code & Fixture Review

A Shop Mentor check-off is mandatory prior to running any program on the **Haas Mini Mill** or **OMAX ProtoMAX Waterjet** to inspect Z-height offsets, garnet abrasive hopper level, and clamp clearance.

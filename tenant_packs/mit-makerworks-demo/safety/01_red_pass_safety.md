# Building N52 Core Safety Mandate

1. **Red Pass Activation**: All makers must tap their campus ID at the N52 mentor station before powering equipment.
2. **Resin Cure Safety**: Uncured Formlabs photopolymer resin is a skin sensitizer; wear nitrile gloves and wash stations must be used before exiting the bay.
